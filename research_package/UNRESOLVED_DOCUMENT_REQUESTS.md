10. CFIUS/ITAR/EAR opinion, foreign-person access matrix, data-flow diagram, and cybersecurity controls.
11. Named auditor, administrator, custodian, counsel, and at least three reference contacts with dated call notes.
12. Litigation, enforcement, sanctions, debarment, bankruptcy, UCC, judgment, and tax-lien searches for principal and each entity.
13. Dated website remediation capture, CMS change record, and disclosure-review policy.
