## Claim reconciliation

### Fund size and capital raised

- A September 2024 With Intelligence report said Outpost Ventures Fund I had received more than $100 million in LP subscriptions, targeted a $150 million final close, and had a $250 million hard cap.
- This pass located a December 2025 Form D for **Outpost Ventures Energy LP**, not a public filing that clearly reconciles the reported Fund I subscriptions.
- Required distinction: soft indications, signed subscriptions, committed capital, accepted subscriptions, called capital, and cash funded.

**Assessment:** Material and unresolved. Obtain bank-supported capital-account evidence and all applicable Form D/ADV filings.

### Government awards and follow-on capital

- Outpost Ventures’ website claims 220+ companies, $480 million-plus in contracts, and $5 billion-plus in follow-on capital.
- A USAFA Association profile cites more than $500 million in U.S. and international government awards for over 250 companies and more than $100 million deployed.
- A 2026 conference biography cites more than $580 million in non-dilutive funding.

**Assessment:** Directionally consistent but not reconciled. The increasing totals may reflect later dates, different populations, or different attribution methods. Require a company-by-company ledger with award identifiers, dates, amounts, Outpost’s role, and duplicate controls.

### Government matching model

Outpost describes every $1 of investment as potentially creating $2–$4 of deployable growth capital. Government programs can leverage private capital, but awards depend on eligibility, competition, agency approvals, appropriations, milestones, and company-specific terms.

**Assessment:** Treat as a scenario, not a guaranteed multiplier. Require each portfolio company’s signed award, matching requirement, funded amount, termination rights, and milestone status.

### Portfolio and performance

Public sources show an active portfolio and third-party participation in financings. Public evidence does not establish fund-level net IRR, TVPI, DPI, realized exits, loss ratio, valuation policy, or audited NAV for the exact offered vehicle.

**Assessment:** Investment performance remains unverified.

## Decision rule

### Proceed

Proceed only when the legal entity, regulatory status, capitalization, cash controls, track record, portfolio valuations, government-award claims, conflicts, and adverse-record representations are independently reconciled without material exceptions.

### Condition or reprice

Condition or reprice if the manager supplies reliable evidence but the vehicle retains emerging-manager, concentration, cross-border, liquidity, key-person, or government-procurement risks. Use staged funding, enhanced reporting, key-person protections, LP advisory rights, valuation controls, and side-letter remedies where appropriate.

### Decline

Decline if the sponsor resists entity-level transparency, requests a wire to a mismatched beneficiary, cannot reconcile capital raised or performance, refuses administrator/auditor verification, relies on promotional government “matching” claims without executed awards, omits related-party economics, or provides materially inconsistent documents.
